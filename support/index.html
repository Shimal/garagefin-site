Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Where is my GarageFin data stored?

Your records are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, they are also stored privately in your iCloud account so they can stay in sync across your own devices.

How do I restore or move my data?

Use GarageFin’s backup and restore tools in Settings. You can also enable iCloud sync on devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

What is the difference between debts and buyer balances?

Debts are standalone amounts you owe or are owed. A buyer balance belongs to a sold vehicle and changes as you record installments in Buyer Payments.

How do partner shares work?

Assign partners to a vehicle and choose percentages or fixed amounts. GarageFin uses those shares to divide the vehicle’s realized profit or loss.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete records and sessions inside the app. You can also remove GarageFin’s iCloud data from your Apple Account settings. Contact us if you need guidance.

Does GarageFin track me or show ads?

No. GarageFin does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking in the current version.
