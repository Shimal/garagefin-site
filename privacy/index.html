Privacy at GarageFin

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information GarageFin handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Information we handleHow it is usedStorage and sharingYour choicesContact

Information we handle

GarageFin handles information you choose to enter, such as vehicle details, financial records, partner and buyer information, debts, notes, photos, and receipts. Profile and business contact details you add may appear on invoices you generate. The app receives only photos and receipts you explicitly select using Apple’s photo picker.

How information is used

Your information is used only to provide GarageFin features, including vehicle tracking, financial calculations, records, reports, invoices, backups, and synchronization between your own devices. It is not used to create advertising profiles or market products to you.

Tracking and analytics

GarageFin does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking in the current version. It does not include a third-party crash-reporting SDK. You may optionally share Apple crash analytics through your device settings.

Storage, synchronization, and sharing

Records are stored on your device. When you enable private iCloud synchronization, information is also stored through Apple’s CloudKit service under your Apple Account. GarageFin does not sell, rent, or share your records with advertisers. Information is sent to NHTSA only when you request VIN decoding, and to a destination you select when you export, share, print, or back up information.

Security

GarageFin relies on iOS, your device passcode or biometric protection when enabled, encrypted network connections, and Apple’s CloudKit security features to help protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and deletion

You control the information you add. You can edit or delete records in the app, disable iCloud synchronization, export a backup, or delete GarageFin data from iCloud through your Apple Account settings. Uninstalling the app removes local app data, but may not remove data already stored in iCloud or backups.

Children’s privacy

GarageFin is a business recordkeeping tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when GarageFin’s features or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new updated date.
